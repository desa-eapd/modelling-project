From 7 to 10 September 2026, the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA) and the United Nations Economic and Social Commission for Western Asia (ESCWA) held a national workshop on macro-fiscal modelling and forecasting for Lebanon. The workshop provided policymakers from Lebanon’s Ministry of Finance with hands-on training using a country-specific extension of UN DESA’s World Economic Forecasting Model (WEFM). UN DESA experts joined remotely.

Sessions covered the model’s structure and data requirements, forecasting and baseline development, and the design and interpretation of policy scenarios. Participants also completed practical exercises using the model and its online interface.

Participants reported that they appreciated the organisation of the workshop and that their conceptual understanding of the MFMF tool and WEFM had improved thanks to the workshop. Feedback also pointed to a gap between improved understanding and confidence in applying the skills.
